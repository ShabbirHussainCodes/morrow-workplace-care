# ADR 0007 — Extras after the core

Status: Accepted (4 Oct 2026)

## Decision
Turnstile, Slack alerts and HubSpot tasks wait until P0–P8 are done. One exception: the free Vercel firewall rate-limit rule is added in P1.

## Why
The core flow and its failure handling are the proof. Extras add surface area without adding proof.
