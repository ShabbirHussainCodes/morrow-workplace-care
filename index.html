How it works

Three steps from enquiry to a clear plan.

  1. 01

    Share your needs

    Tell us about your space, how it is used and when you need support. A short form is all it takes.

  2. 02

    Walk through the space

    We visit, see how the space works day to day and note the areas that matter most to your team.

  3. 03

    Agree on a plan

    You get a written scope, a visit schedule and one named contact. Changes are agreed in writing, not guessed.
